The Editor-in-Chief is the heart and voice behind PianoEd. With years of experience both teaching and playing, they believe the piano belongs to everyone — not just prodigies and conservatory students.
Their writing spans all five content pillars, from the rich history of the instrument to practical technique tips, gear reviews, and the latest developments in piano technology. They bring a warmth and accessibility that makes even the most complex topics feel approachable.
Above all, they believe that a love for the piano is something to be shared, celebrated, and nurtured — whether you're playing your first chord or your thousandth sonata.
