The History Writer is PianoEd's resident storyteller, weaving the rich tapestry of the piano's past into vivid, engaging narratives. From the workshops of Cristofori to the concert halls of today, they bring the instrument's journey to life.
Their work spans artist profiles, cultural deep dives, and repertoire explorations across all genres — treating classical, jazz, pop, and contemporary with equal respect and curiosity. They believe every piece of music has a story worth telling, and every pianist has left a mark worth remembering.
Their writing is heartfelt and accessible, inviting readers to fall in love with the piano not just as an instrument, but as a cultural force that has shaped centuries of human expression.
