Written by Prince and immortalized by Sinéad O'Connor's breathtaking 1990 vocal performance, *"Nothing Compares 2 U"* stands as one of the most poignant emotional ballads in contemporary music. With its sparse harmonic pacing, melancholy chord suspensions, and wide dynamic breadth, the song is an exceptional study piece for beginner pianists learning expressive phrasing and tone control.

This slow-tempo tutorial strips away complex studio synthesizers to provide a clean, acoustic piano arrangement that you can play with nuance, even if you are just starting your keyboard journey.

## Chord chart

The arrangement is centered in the key of **F Major** (1 flat: `Bb`), creating a warm, comforting tonal foundation. For beginners seeking to avoid black keys entirely, transposing to **C Major** is an effortless alternative, but learning the standard F Major version is fantastic for building finger dexterity across both white and black key surfaces.

### Primary Chords (F Major)
* **F Major (`F`)**: `F - A - C`
* **C/E (C Major First Inversion)**: `E - G - C`
* **Dm (D Minor)**: `D - F - A`
* **Bb (B-flat Major)**: `Bb - D - F`
* **Csus4 (C Suspended 4th)**: `C - F - G`
* **C (C Major)**: `C - E - G`
* **Am (A Minor)**: `A - C - E`
* **Gm (G Minor)**: `G - Bb - D`

### Stepwise Descending Bassline
The hallmark of the verse is the descending bassline: `F → E → D → Bb`. This stepwise downward motion evokes deep longing and introspection, guiding the listener through each line of the lyric.

## Section-by-section

To practice efficiently, divide your practice session into distinct musical zones:

### 1. The Verse ("It's been seven hours and fifteen days...")
The verse progression consists of four 4-beat measures played at a slow, meditative tempo (around 60 BPM):
* Measure 1: `F` (4 beats)
* Measure 2: `C/E` (4 beats) — play low `E` in the bass with `C - E - G` in the right hand
* Measure 3: `Dm` (4 beats)
* Measure 4: `Bb` (4 beats)

Keep the right-hand chords gently pulsating on beats 1, 2, 3, and 4 in quarter notes, maintaining an even, soothing volume level.

### 2. The Pre-Chorus ("Since you took your love away...")
Here, the harmony introduces subtle tension before resolving:
* Measure 1: `Bb` (2 beats) → `Am` (2 beats)
* Measure 2: `Gm` (2 beats) → `Csus4` (2 beats)
* Measure 3: `C` (hold 4 beats to let the suspended 4th resolve into the major 3rd)

<Callout>
**Musical Insight**: Listen carefully to the shift from `Csus4` to `C`. The `F` note softly resolves down to `E`. Highlight that internal voice movement in your right hand—it is the emotional hinge of the pre-chorus.
</Callout>

### 3. The Chorus ("'Cause nothing compares...")
The chorus broadens into powerful root-position statements:
* Measure 1: `Bb` (4 beats: "Nothing compares...")
* Measure 2: `Am` (4 beats: "Nothing compares...")
* Measure 3: `Gm` (2 beats) → `C` (2 beats: "...2 U")
* Measure 4: `F` (4 beats resolution)

## Pedal tips

Because *"Nothing Compares 2 U"* is played at a slow tempo with long-held chords, improper pedaling is the number one cause of muddy sound. Keep these three pedal guidelines in mind:

1. **Use Syncopated (Legato) Pedaling**: Depress the sustain pedal *immediately after* striking the chord keys, not before or at the exact same instant. 
2. **Clear on Every Root Change**: When the left hand moves from `F` to `E` (in `C/E`) or `E` to `D` (in `Dm`), lift the pedal completely to prevent low-end resonance buildup.
3. **Half-Pedaling on Acoustic Pianos**: If playing on an acoustic upright or grand, experiment with pressing the damper pedal only half-way down. This dampens extraneous overtone ring while maintaining warmth and sustain.

## Sheet music

When selecting sheet music for this piece, seek out slow-tempo beginner arrangements that provide:
* Large, clear notation with finger numbering on octave shifts
* Chord symbols printed directly above the vocal melody staff
* Full lyrical accompaniment to help track your position throughout extended verses
