Charlie Puth is renowned throughout the modern music industry for his extraordinary perfect pitch and hyper-meticulous pop craftsmanship. When he joined forces with BTS vocalist Jung Kook on the chart-dominating single *"Left and Right"*, the result was an infectious, rhythmic masterclass built around punchy syncopated guitar plucks, stereo-panned vocal harmonies, and irresistible upbeat energy.

Adapting *"Left and Right"* for solo piano provides an exhilarating rhythmic workout. It teaches beginner and intermediate players how to translate driving studio pop grooves into crisp, percussive keyboard playing.

## Chords

The track is set in the vibrant key of **Eb Major (E-flat Major)** with a tempo of approximately **106 BPM**. While playing with three flats (`Bb`, `Eb`, `Ab`) might seem intimidating to absolute beginners, the progression remains identical across the entire song, cycling continuously through four primary chords:

1. **Ab Major (`Ab` or `IV`)**: `Ab - C - Eb`
2. **Eb Major (`Eb` or `I`)**: `Eb - G - Bb`
3. **Bb Major (`Bb` or `V`)**: `Bb - D - F`
4. **Cm (C Minor or `vi`)**: `C - Eb - G`

### Chord voicings and smooth hand movement
To prevent awkward hand jumps between high and low registers, use compact inversions around the middle register:
* **Ab Major**: Play root position `Ab3 - C4 - Eb4`
* **Eb Major**: Play second inversion `Bb3 - Eb4 - G4`
* **Bb Major**: Play first inversion `D4 - F4 - Bb4`
* **Cm**: Play root position `C4 - Eb4 - G4`

<Callout>
**Practice Tip for Beginners**: Notice how `Eb4` is shared between `Ab` and `Eb`, and `G4` is shared between `Eb` and `Cm`. Keeping these common tones stationary under the same fingers allows your hands to glide between chords with minimal physical exertion.
</Callout>

## Melody

Charlie Puth's vocal melody in *"Left and Right"* is punchy, staccato, and centered around short, memorable interval leaps. 

### The Iconic Hook Phrase ("Memories follow me left and right...")
The main hook begins just after the downbeat on the "and" of beat 1:
* *"Memories follow me..."*: `Eb4 - Eb4 - G4 - Bb4 - Bb4 - C5`
* *"...left and right, I can feel you over here..."*: `Bb4 - Ab4 - G4 - F4 - Eb4 - F4 - G4`
* *"...I can feel you over here, you take up every corner of my mind"*: Rapid syllabic repetitions on `Eb4` stepping up to `G4` and resolving to `Bb4`.

When articulating this melody, avoid using heavy sustain pedal. Puth's production style thrives on tight, dry percussive attacks. Practice lifting your fingers cleanly off each key to replicate the crispness of his vocal delivery.

## Rhythm

The true heartbeat of *"Left and Right"* is its syncopated 16th-note rhythm. Unlike traditional classical music where downbeats dominate, pop funk relies on rhythmic anticipation:

### Left-Hand Staccato Bass Plucks
Instead of holding long sustained bass notes, strike short, bouncy eighth-note pulses:
* **Beat 1**: Strike the low root note (`Ab2`) sharply with finger 5 (pinky)
* **Beat 2 "and"**: Strike an octave chord or fifth (`Eb3`)
* **Beat 3**: Mute or play a gentle ghost note on the wood or deadened keybed
* **Beat 4 "and"**: Anticipate the next measure by pushing into the upcoming root

### The "Left and Right" Stereo Illusion on Piano
In the original mix, the vocals alternate rapidly between left and right stereo channels. You can emulate this acoustic illusion on piano by splitting your registers:
* Play the "left" response phrase in the mid-low register (around Octave 3)
* Play the "right" echo phrase two octaves higher (in Octave 5) with a lighter touch

This dynamic contrast captivates listeners and adds an element of theatrical showmanship to your live performances.
