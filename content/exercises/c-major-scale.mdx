# C Major Scale (Two Octaves)

The **C Major scale** contains only the natural white keys: **C – D – E – F – G – A – B – C**. While simple on the page, performing two fluid octaves hands together requires mastered thumb crossings (*passaggio*) and relaxed lateral wrist shifts.

## Fingering Architecture

The standard piano fingering for scales is designed so your hand never runs out of fingers while ascending or descending.

### Right Hand Fingering (Ascending)
`1 - 2 - 3` (thumb tucks under on F) `1 - 2 - 3 - 4` (thumb tucks under on high C) `1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5`

### Left Hand Fingering (Ascending)
`5 - 4 - 3 - 2 - 1` (finger 3 crosses over to A) `3 - 2 - 1` (finger 4 crosses over to F) `4 - 3 - 2 - 1`

## The Secret to Smooth Scale Playing

* **The Glide Principle**: Your hand should move smoothly like a train on a track. Avoid jerky, bouncing wrist motions when crossing fingers.
* **Weight Transfer**: Transfer the weight of your arm from fingertip to fingertip rather than pressing down with pure finger muscle force alone.
