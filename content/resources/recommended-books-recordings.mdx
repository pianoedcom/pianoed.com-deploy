# Recommended Books & Recordings
A curated collection of the best piano books, method books, recordings, and online courses — organized by genre and skill level. Every recommendation here is something we've used and loved.
## Method Books for Beginners
### Adult Piano Adventures (Nancy and Randall Faber)
The most popular adult method book. Structured, progressive, and includes theory alongside repertoire. Book 1 takes you from zero to playing simple arrangements of real pieces.
### Alfred's Adult All-in-One Course
A comprehensive method that combines lessons, theory, and technique in a single book. Slightly more traditional than Faber, with a focus on classical repertoire.
### John Thompson's Modern Course for the Piano
A classic method book series that's been used for generations. More demanding than Faber or Alfred, but excellent for students who want a thorough, traditional approach.
## Classical Piano Books
### Bach: Two-Part Inventions (Henle Verlag)
The urtext edition of Bach's 15 inventions. Essential for developing hand independence and contrapuntal thinking. Start with No. 1 in C Major.
### Chopin: Nocturnes (Paderewski Edition)
The complete Nocturnes — some of the most beautiful music ever written for piano. The Paderewski edition is the standard reference for Chopin.
### Beethoven: 32 Sonatas (ABRSM)
The complete Beethoven sonatas in a scholarly edition. The "Moonlight," "Pathétique," and "Appassionata" are essential listening and playing.
## Jazz Piano Books
### The Jazz Piano Book (Mark Levine)
The definitive jazz piano reference. Covers everything from basic voicings to advanced reharmonization. Every jazz pianist should own this.
### Jazz Piano Voicings for the Non-Pianist (Mike Tracy)
Teaches the essential left-hand voicings that every jazz pianist needs. Perfect for instrumentalists who want to comp for themselves.
### Metaphors for the Musician (Randy Halberstadt)
An advanced guide to improvisation and comping. Deep, thoughtful, and full of practical exercises.
## Essential Recordings
### Classical
- **Glenn Gould: Bach Goldberg Variations** (1981) — A landmark recording that redefined Bach interpretation.
- **Arthur Rubinstein: Chopin Nocturnes** — The warmest, most noble Chopin on record.
- **Vladimir Horowitz: The Last Recording** — Horowitz's final sessions, deeply moving.
### Jazz
- **Bill Evans: Sunday at the Village Vanguard** — The definitive piano trio recording.
- **Thelonious Monk: Solo Monk** — Monk alone at the piano, pure and unfiltered.
- **Oscar Peterson: Night Train** — Swinging, bluesy, and technically dazzling.
## Online Courses
### Pianote
A subscription-based online piano school with structured lessons for all levels. Excellent production quality and engaging teachers.
### Flowkey
An app-based learning platform that listens to your playing and provides real-time feedback. Great for learning specific songs.
### Simply Piano
A gamified learning app for absolute beginners. Fun, accessible, and a good starting point for those who are unsure if piano is for them.
