# Sheet Music Library
Welcome to the PianoEd Sheet Music Library — a curated, annotated collection of sheet music recommendations across all genres. Whether you're looking for your first classical piece, jazz standards to jam with, or pop arrangements to play for fun, you'll find something here.
## Classical Sheet Music
### Beginner Level
- **Bach: Minuet in G Major (BWV Anh. 114)** — The perfect first classical piece. Simple, beautiful, and teaches two-voice coordination.
- **Beethoven: Ode to Joy** — Uses only five notes. Instantly recognizable and deeply satisfying for beginners.
- **Clementi: Sonatina Op. 36 No. 1** — The gateway to classical sonatinas. Light, elegant, and pedagogically excellent.
### Intermediate Level
- **Chopin: Prelude in E Minor (Op. 28 No. 4)** — Technically accessible, musically profound. A masterclass in expressive playing.
- **Bach: Two-Part Invention No. 1 in C Major** — The gold standard for developing hand independence.
- **Mozart: Sonata in C Major (K. 545)** — The "easy" sonata that's actually quite demanding musically.
## Jazz Sheet Music
### The Real Book (6th Edition)
The definitive jazz fake book. Contains hundreds of standards with accurate melodies and chord changes. Essential for any jazz pianist.
### Jazz Piano Solos
- **Bill Evans: Waltz for Debby** — A beautiful, accessible jazz piano piece.
- **Thelonious Monk: Round Midnight** — The most recorded jazz standard. Complex but rewarding.
- **Oscar Peterson: Hymn to Freedom** — A powerful, soulful piece that's surprisingly approachable.
## Pop Piano Sheet Music
### Easy Pop Arrangements
- **Adele: Someone Like You** — Simple chord progression, powerful melody.
- **The Beatles: Let It Be** — Iconic, accessible, and teaches the I-V-vi-IV progression.
- **John Legend: All of Me** — Beautiful melody with manageable accompaniment.
## Where to Download Free Sheet Music
<Callout type="info">
**IMSLP.org** is the world's largest public domain sheet music library. Everything published before 1929 is free to download. It's the first stop for any classical pianist.
</Callout>
